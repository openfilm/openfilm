# OpenFilm's library

The transitions, effects, text, stickers and sounds in this folder are made by the OpenFilm contributors: the pages
written by hand, the sounds made by code (`scripts/library-sounds.mjs`), the pictures drawn by OpenFilm's renderer
(`scripts/library-previews.mjs`) over stills made for OpenFilm.

They are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): use
them in any film, commercial or not, change them, share them, with no need to credit anyone.
