A line of words from the library goes in with a box that hugs the words, and on the picture it is one thing: pointed at, hovered, pressed or dragged, it is its whole clip with one frame, and a double-click still goes in to type. The line's ways of coming in, moving and going out stay within the room its box keeps around the words, so they are never cut by the box.
