Show a circular loading indicator while library assets load and offer retry when a download fails, load sticker animations only on click, and use the viewer's transport for asset previews. Clicking the timeline restores the project viewer. Keep the add button visible while cached media opens, and add assets with the button, keyboard or drag. Hover on a visual card to preview it in place. Template previews share one cached video between the card and viewer. Moving away stops playback but leaves downloads running. Audio previews start on click. Voices show their download status in the same position as the use button, and sticker cards are larger. Voice previews animate the colors of each voice’s avatar in the viewer. Sounds and music show their own artwork. Clicking empty space dismisses the selected preview.
