Canvas: Chinese and other input methods write in a text node and a node's prompt as typed (the box no longer drops what is being composed, leaving pinyin behind). A text node is moved by dragging anywhere on it; a double click, Enter or simply typing while it is picked writes in it. Its words are drawn as Markdown: headings, bulleted, numbered and to-do lists, quotes, code, bold, italic, strikethrough and rules, with a list button in its bar. The redraw dialog has its own keys (⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z, [ ], E, B) and the board under it takes none. The board's dots are visible at every zoom, Beta sits at its bottom left, and nodes are listed in the order a film is made: text, image, audio, video, cut.
