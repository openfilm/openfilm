Studio: the captions of what is said make themselves. Once a voice with a transcript is in the film (a voice made with its words timed has one), a caption track of it is put on top, with nothing transcribed again. While no one changes those captions by hand they follow the film: a voice moved, trimmed or put in, and they are written again in their look. Captions changed by hand are left as they are, and "Make again" in the library's Captions makes them follow again. A caption track of speech the person took out is not put back.
