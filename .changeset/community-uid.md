Every OpenFilm account has a UID, a number that never changes, and a maker's page in the Community shows it beside their name, as does your own when you share. A page can be found by its UID as well as its name. A name in the Community now needs at least one letter, since digits alone are a UID; a mail address of digits alone suggests a name that starts with "u".
