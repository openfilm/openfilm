An element of a page clip can move by keyframes: an override's `style.animation` plays the film's own `@keyframes`, which the host puts in the page, on the page's own time like the page's own animations. Copies of the clip get keyframes of their own.

In Studio's Motion, a page clip double-clicked opens as a composition (‹ film / clip): its elements are its layers, nested as in the page (the Parent column), each shown or hidden (👁), blended, and twirled open to its position, scale, rotation and opacity with stopwatches, values in blue and keyframes on their rows, kept in the clip's overrides.
