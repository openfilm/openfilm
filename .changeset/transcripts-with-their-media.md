A video's or sound's transcript (and its translations) beside it is no longer listed in the media drawer as a file of its own: the media is marked instead, and its inspector shows the transcript's lines with their times. Nothing on disk changes, and a transcript still moves and is renamed with its media. A WebVTT with no media of its name is still listed, and previews as its lines. The drawer's player stands on the chat's composer again, and a side bar heading has a light fill only while the line shown is under it.
