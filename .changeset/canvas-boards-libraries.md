Canvas: a project can have several canvases (new, rename, duplicate, delete into the trash, open in a new window), and each can be seen as a storyboard of its material by kind. Generation history lists everything the canvases made, in this project or every project, ready to put back on the board. A character library keeps characters (portrait, full body, expressions, turnaround) for every project: generate one from a few words, keep a group or a picture as one, and wire it into any picture or clip. Style and effects libraries put ready-made words for a look or a camera move on the board, with favorites and recently used. The model list shows how long each model took you and what it takes.
