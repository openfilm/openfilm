Motion shares timeline interactions with Edit, including the ruler, clip dragging, trimming and selection. In Motion, each picture clip has its own layer row, with switches in the header and keyframes under the row. A composition opened from a page shows the page's elements the same way. The edit keeps out of keyframes: no keyframe rows under its tracks, no stopwatches in its inspector, no motion path on its picture.

Enable Motion in the editor catalog and switch to it from the top-right menu.
