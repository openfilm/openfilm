Motion is drawn by the edit's own timeline: the same toolbar, ruler, clips, drags, trims and selection, each picture clip on a row of its own as a layer (its switches and mode in its head), its properties opening under its row with their keyframes. A composition opened from a page shows the page's elements the same way. The edit keeps out of keyframes: no keyframe rows under its tracks, no stopwatches in its inspector, no motion path on its picture.

Settings → Advanced says which ways of working a project offers (edit, canvas, motion); with one on, the top bar has no switch. The switch's menu is a short list of them, the one in use in the accent, with a way to those settings.
