"openfilm": patch
---

A person who speaks in a generated clip speaks with its own sound, the voice and the lips made together. `openfilm get video --voice <recording>` gives whose voice speaks the words written in the prompt (Seedance, at OpenRouter and fal), so a person keeps one voice across clips; `--audio <recording>` makes a person speak that recording word for word, the clip as long as it (fal: OmniHuman v2 or Kling Avatar v2 from a picture of the person, sync-3 for a clip's lips). Its help says to never lay a voice over a clip made without it; Gemini Omni and OpenRouter say where a clip speaking a recording is made.
