Effects that move: a clip's (or a page element's) `filter` can read the effects' custom properties (`--fx-blur`, `--fx-glow`, `--fx-brightness`, `--fx-contrast`, `--fx-saturate`, `--fx-grayscale`, `--fx-hue`), which the host registers, so each eases by keyframes of its own (SPEC §1).

In Studio's Motion, a layer twirled open has an Effects group as an animation editor's: Gaussian blur, brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, black & white and glow, added from its menu, each a row with its navigator, stopwatch, value in blue and keyframes (E shows them alone, U the moving ones too), on clips and on a composition's elements alike.
