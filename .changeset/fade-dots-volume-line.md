Studio: a selected clip on the timeline has a small round handle at each end, where its sound starts (on top of a video's sound, or of a sound clip's waveform). Drag one inward to fade the clip in or out; the fade is drawn as a slope from the handle down to the clip's corner, and on a video the faded picture darkens toward the edge. A thin line across the clip's sound is its volume: drag it up to make the clip louder or down to make it quieter, with the level shown as you drag, one undo step. The line sits a little above the middle when the clip plays as is, and stays quiet until you point at it. While Speed is on, the handles at the clip's ends are filled with the accent color and its edges stay lit.
