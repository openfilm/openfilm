Studio: the clip under the pointer on the timeline has a small round handle just inside each end, on the bottom of its picture (where a video's sound starts, or the bottom of a page or a still) or on the top of a sound clip's waveform. Drag one inward to fade the clip in or out; where the handle sits is where the fade ends, and nothing else is drawn for it. A hairline across the clip's sound is its volume: drag it up to make the clip louder or down to make it quieter, with the level shown as you drag, one undo step. The line sits a little above the middle when the clip plays as is, and is barely there until you point at it. The handles and the line show only while the pointer is on the clip, selected or not, and stay while one is dragged; a selected clip the pointer is not on, or clips selected with a box, show none. A clip is trimmed by dragging its lit edge itself: no bar shows on its edges any more. While Speed is on, dragging an edge changes the speed, and the clip's frame turns the accent color to say so.
