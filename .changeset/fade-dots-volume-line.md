Studio: a selected clip on the timeline has a small round handle just inside each end, where its sound starts (on top of a video's sound, or of a sound clip's waveform). Drag one inward to fade the clip in or out; the fade is drawn as a slope from the handle down to the clip's corner, and on a video the faded picture darkens toward the edge. A hairline across the clip's sound is its volume: drag it up to make the clip louder or down to make it quieter, with the level shown as you drag, one undo step. The line sits a little above the middle when the clip plays as is, and is barely there until you point at it. A clip is trimmed by dragging its lit edge itself: no bar shows on its edges any more. While Speed is on, dragging an edge changes the speed, and the clip's frame turns the accent color to say so.
