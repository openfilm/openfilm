Motion's toolbar has an animation editor's tools in the place of cutting's: Easy Ease on the picked keyframes (F9), the graph editor (⇧F3) and what moves on the picked layers (U), with undo, markers, snapping and zoom; no modes, split, magnet or linkage. A click between layers picks no gap there, and the edit's picked gap is a light wash, not a dashed box.
