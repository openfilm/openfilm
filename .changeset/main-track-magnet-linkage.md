Studio's timeline has a main track again: the lowest picture track, drawn taller, with switches in the toolbar. The magnet keeps it closed up (a clip put on it goes in between, a deleted one closes the gap, a trim or a new speed moves what follows). Linkage keeps the titles, overlays and sounds over a main-track clip on their moment of it, and deletes them with it; a clip that starts past the end of the main track rides on nothing. Dragging the cut between two clips trims the clip you grab. The preview axis shows the frame under the pointer as it moves over the timeline. Keys: P (or ⇧N) magnet, ~ linkage, S preview axis.
