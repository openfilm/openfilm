Canvas: going from the edit to the canvas and back is seamless. Once opened, the canvas stays as it was left (its view, what is selected, what is open), hidden under the edit, instead of loading and fitting its board again each time; the first time it shows, it shows already fitted. ⌘E (Ctrl+E) switches between the edit and the canvas from either, set in Settings › Keyboard shortcuts with the others. The shortcuts panel sits in the middle of the board's foot, with the switch in it. The list of what is on the board filters by kind (pictures, videos, sounds, text, groups) as well as by words. A made picture carries no "AI" tag, and a making node says "Inputs changed" only when what is wired into it changed since it made one, not when its own words or settings did.
