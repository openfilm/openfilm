Studio: a click on a layer, a clip or something in the picture only selects it. The small bar of buttons that came up beside the selection is gone, and everything it offered is in the right-click menu, which is complete everywhere. With a chat beside Studio, Reference in chat is always the first item, then a line, then the rest. A row of the Layers panel gives the same menu as that layer in the picture, and selects it; a clip's row gives the clip's menu. In the picture, a clip's menu has Crop and Mask, and several layers' Align has Distribute. On the timeline, a clip's menu has Duplicate, Mute or Unmute, and Speed (checked while on), which makes dragging the clip's edges change its speed instead of trimming it; Speed is also a button beside Split in the timeline's toolbar. With a range marked, the ruler's menu has Play range and Clear range. A right-click menu closes on any click outside it (the Layers panel's stayed open).
