A new workbench, Screening, for people who have never edited: the film plays large, as it will be exported, and pointing at what to change takes one gesture — a click on the crosshair for this moment, a drag on the picture to circle a part, a drag on the strip below to pick a stretch of picture, sound or captions, a round button on a shot, a sound or a caption, or the whole film. Each goes into the chat, numbered there and on the film, and its × takes it out again. The strip shows the shots as a filmstrip, the sounds as waveforms and the captions; hovering shows the shot and the time. When the agent changes the film, Screening says how many places changed and plays them one after another. Holding ⌘ (Ctrl on Windows) shows its shortcuts.
