A new workbench, Screening, for people who have never edited: the film plays large, as it will be exported, and plain tools point at what to change (this moment, a part of the picture circled, a stretch, a shot, a sound, a caption, the whole film), each put in the chat for the person to say what they want there. A strip under the picture shows the film's shots, sounds and captions in order; a click goes there. When the agent changes the film, Screening says how many places changed and plays them one after another. Without the app's chat, a pointer is copied as a link to paste to an agent. Files dropped on it go into the project. Nothing in it edits the film.
