A new workbench, Screening, for people who have never edited: the film plays large, as it will be exported, with one bar under it — play, the time, the whole film as a strip of its frames, the speed — and pointing at what to change is one gesture: the crosshair (or M) for this moment, a drag on the picture to circle a part, a drag on the strip to pick a stretch. Each goes into the chat, numbered there and on the film, and its × takes it out again. Hovering the strip shows the frame there. When the agent changes the film, Screening says how many places changed and plays them one after another. Holding ⌘ (Ctrl on Windows) shows its shortcuts. It comes with a skill that has agents treat what is pointed at as a director's notes: change only that, never render a video just to show it, and link each change.

The workbenches are now side by side on the top bar, each a click away; ⌥1, ⌥2… (Alt on Windows) show them in order and ⌘E (Ctrl+E) goes back to the one before.
