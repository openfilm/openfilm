Canvas: GPT Image 2.6's own abilities on the board. A picture node can be made as a GIF (its frames and each frame's length) or an SVG; any picture can be changed in part — brush the area to redraw, or draw the change on it in a color that stands out — and the change is made as a new picture beside it. The model list says which models do these. Empty cards are one centered composition, their starts as pills; the settings popovers are lighter, a slider for lengths and frames.
