Studio: a video your browser can’t decode, such as HEVC from a DJI drone or a phone on a PC without an HEVC decoder, no longer shows a black picture. The viewer says “Preparing this clip to play… 42%” over it while Studio makes a copy it can play, and the media pane shows the same. These copies are made before any other, even with proxies turned off in Settings, and the picture appears as soon as one is ready, without reloading. A copy that can’t be made says why. Exports still use the original.
