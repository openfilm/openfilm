Studio: the resource center lists only places connected from outside OpenFilm, with Projects and the asset library fixed first in its sidebar, and files used from them under recent; a new folder is made in the library itself. A view of no one folder offers no import, and adding to a collection is on a file's right-click menu. Settings → Storage shows what OpenFilm takes: projects and linked projects (media, versions, conversations, files), the library, skills, exports, cache and the app's own folders, measured once and refreshed after a clear. The add-to-project choices use Studio's menus, and the chat's way back sits on the drawer without shading it.
