Studio: a talking head exported to 剪映, CapCut, Premiere or Resolve stays one clip with its own sound. A video filling the frame (`object-fit: cover`, framed anywhere by `object-position`) is placed by the editor's own scale and position instead of being drawn into a new file with its sound split off beside it, and a style that only says what CSS does anyway (`object-fit: contain`, `opacity: 1`, as choosing Fit in the inspector writes) no longer counts as a look of its own. A fill its box cuts inside the frame, a stretch and any other look are drawn as before.
