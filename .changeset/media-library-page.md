A media library, beside My projects and Examples in the projects window: the place to look through the media you
keep on this computer, whether or not a film uses it. Add the folders you keep footage, pictures and sound in and
they show as they are on your disk, with their own folders to open, at once however large they are; nothing is copied
or moved, and a folder on a disk that is not plugged in says so. Move the pointer across a video to look through it.
Pick a file to play it and see what it is, where it is, which of your projects use it (under whatever name they have
it) and, for media a service made, the prompt and model. Each project's media is listed there too.
