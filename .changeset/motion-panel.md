The workspace switch on the top bar is one button with a fill of its own (no arrow): it opens a card of Edit, Canvas and Motion, each with what it is for, the one in use in the accent.

Motion has its own panel in the inspector's place: Properties (the picked layer's Transform, then its effects, 3D and stroke as sliders, each with a stopwatch) and Effects & Presets (animation presets in, out and all along a layer, effects, mask shapes, text animators, 3D and a stroke drawn on, each a tile that plays what it does under the pointer: clicked, it goes on the picked layers; dragged, onto a layer of the timeline). On the motion timeline a layer shows only the groups it has; the rest is put on it from its menu (a right click, or its +), and each group twirls open and shut and can be taken off.
