Settings › Services is one page. Every service is a chip at the top, OpenFilm first and the connected ones ahead of the rest; a click opens its card, with Connect, how it is paid, and every kind of media with the models it makes it with or that it does not. Each kind of media's menu lists the connected services' models, and under them the others, each a Connect that opens its card. Add a provider says what a built-in one has to be, and that any service works today as a custom service. Translating subtitles, looking at pictures and searching the web are left to the agent's own model. Providers is no longer a page of its own.
