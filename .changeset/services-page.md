Settings › Services is one page. Every service is a chip at the top, OpenFilm first and the connected ones ahead of the rest; a click opens its card: who it is, Connect, every kind it could make with its models, and how it is paid. Each kind's menu lists every service that makes it with its models, the ones not connected opening their card. Add a provider, under More, says what a built-in one has to be and opens a custom service's form. Web search sits in the same list, for agents without their own; translating subtitles and looking at pictures are left to the agent's own model. Providers is no longer a page of its own.
