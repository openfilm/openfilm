Media a service made for the project now says how it was made. Pick a generated picture, clip, sound, piece of music
or voice-over in the media pane and the inspector shows the prompt (copied with a click), the service and model, the
rest of what was asked, and the files it was made from; something found on the web says whose it is and links its
page. "Make another" asks for the same thing once more, under a new name. It is kept by the file's content, so it
stays when the file is renamed or moved, and All media shows it for a file of another project.

Files asked for the same way, and a picture redrawn from one of them, are versions of one thing: the media pane shows
them as one tile with their count, the newest on top, and the inspector lists them all to go back to any.

Searching the media pane now finds generated media by the words of its prompt, in this project and in All media.

This covers what is made from now on, by your agent's `openfilm get`, the canvas or the voices pane. What a canvas
made before says its prompt, service and model where the canvas noted them; other media made before has no record.
