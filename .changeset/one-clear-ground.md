Pictures, videos and pages that are clear in places show it the same way everywhere, on a faint checkerboard in either theme: a picture's or a video's poster with an alpha is kept as a PNG, and the preview lays them out at their own shape. Something dragged from the library or the media drawer becomes the clip it will be as soon as it is over the timeline, solid where it lands, its picture no longer following the pointer there. The drawer's player floats as a card above the composer, and a heading in the drawer's side bar that folds lines of its own has a light fill.
