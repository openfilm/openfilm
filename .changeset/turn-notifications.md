While OpenFilm is not in front, the system says when the chat's agent has finished a turn, stopped with an error, or waits for your answer (a question or a permission). Clicking the notification brings the window back on that project's tab; the taskbar button flashes on Windows and the Dock icon bounces on macOS. Settings › General › Notifications switches them off. An error is shown without your home folder, keys or email addresses.
