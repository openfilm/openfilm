Chat: `@` is the one menu: moments, files and skills. Every skill is there with what each does, and picking one names it in the message, as the Skills button does. Typing `/` opens nothing: the agent's own commands (`/review`, `/mcp`…) are no longer listed, and one typed by hand still goes to the agent as typed. With many files, the skills after them keep their rows, and the arrow keys keep the row they are on in view.
