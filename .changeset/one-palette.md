Studio and the app's chat have one palette, light and dark, on a cool gray. Backgrounds step up plainly from the window to the panes to what is raised over them (cards, menus, dialogs), with fields, hover, pressed and selected the same on every layer; text has three levels and lines two. The main actions are ink, near black in light and near white in dark; one teal accent marks what is selected, current or focused: switches, progress, tabs, the timeline's toggles and the frame on the picture. On the timeline, clips lie on the panel with no rows drawn, each a quiet color by kind (video, image, page, text and captions, and one for every sound) under white words, with its waveform drawn lighter over it; the playhead and a picked clip's frame are white (ink in light), and a track is no longer lit when it is picked or aimed at. A track's sound switch is a pair of headphones, and solo is a plain S.
