Studio and the app's chat have one palette, light and dark, on a cool gray. Backgrounds step up plainly from the window to the panes to what is raised over them (cards, menus, dialogs), with fields, hover, pressed and selected the same on every layer; text has three levels and lines two. The main actions are ink, near black in light and near white in dark; one teal accent marks what is selected, current or focused: switches, progress, tabs, the timeline's toggles, the playhead and the frame on the picture. Clips on the timeline each have a hue of their own by kind (video, image, page, text and captions, voice, effects, music), which tints the clip, colors its name and draws its waveform. A track's sound switch is a pair of headphones, and solo is a plain S.
