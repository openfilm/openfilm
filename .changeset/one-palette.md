Studio and the app's chat have one palette, light and dark. Backgrounds step up plainly from the window to the panes to what is raised over them (cards, menus, dialogs), with fields, hover, pressed and selected the same on every layer; text has three levels and lines two. One teal accent marks what is selected, current or focused and the main actions, in both themes: buttons, switches, progress, tabs, the playhead and the frame on the picture. Clips on the timeline each have a color of their own by kind (video, image, page, text and captions, voice, effects, music), and their waveforms show on each.
