Finding things in the media library. A search box looks through every place at once, by a file's name, the folders
it is in, a project's name, and for media a service made, the words of its prompt; each file found says where it is,
and "Show its folder" goes there. Show only video, pictures or sound, in a folder or in what was found. Two views of
the whole library sit under your places: Newest, and Generated (what a service made, in any project).
