Music gives way to speech. A clip with `duck` in film.html is lowered while someone speaks and comes back after:
`duck` alone lowers it 12 dB, `duck="8"` by 8. Someone speaks where a sound or a video has words beside it (its
`.vtt`), and all along a voice-over without words. Music put on the timeline in Studio gives way from the start; the
Sound tab of the inspector turns it off or sets how deep, and the timeline draws the clip's volume dipping under the
speech. The preview, every render and the sound export hear the same; stems stay as they are.

"Level all voices" (a clip's menu → Normalize loudness) brings every voice clip of the film to the same loudness, as
volumes you can change by hand after.
