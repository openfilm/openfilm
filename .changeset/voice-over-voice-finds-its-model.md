A voice-over is spoken by a model that has its voice. A voice of another service goes to that service's model, through OpenFilm's service or a key the person has connected: a Doubao voice is no longer sent to ElevenLabs. A voice's name, such as "ShanShan" for Shan Shan, or an id cut short becomes the voice's id. A private voice may be named as Studio shows it. When no model here can speak the voice, or --model names one that cannot, `openfilm get tts` says which can, and nothing is sent or spent.
