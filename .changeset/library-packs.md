The library and the Community work as one, with packs. A pack is things put together: a music pack of forty pieces, or a mix of music, transitions and text looks. OpenFilm's own groups are its packs, always in the library; the community's are added whole from their page, where each thing can be heard or seen, starred or used on its own, and the whole pack fetched for working offline. An added pack is a line of its own in each library tab it has things in, under Added packs; a thing of the community is in the library once it or its pack is added, and taken out again from Mine. Every library tab ends with Find more in Community, and a right click on a thing opens its page there (its preview, the pack it is in). Packs can be starred, kept with the library's stars. Community catalogs may list packs (`packs`), each one maker's things in their order; older catalogs are read as before.
