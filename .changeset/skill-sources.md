Discover sorts skills by who made them: All, OpenFilm or Community. A community skill shows its maker's name on its cover and on its page ("Community · by WEBGRS", linking to their account). It comes after OpenFilm's own, in the order the index lists it. A skill's page says what it needs beyond OpenFilm and which computers it runs on, and a skill that can't run on this computer says so and can't be installed. The page also counts the scripts a skill brings, which run on your computer, and marks a skill added from a link as not reviewed by OpenFilm. Installed skills are grouped as OpenFilm's and the community's. `openfilm.json` gains `needs` and `platforms`.
