The preview opens, scrubs and plays a long film faster. Footage whose key frames are far apart (a phone's or a camera's H.264, a screen recording, a generated clip) now plays in the preview from a light copy made in the background, the most used footage first, so a scrub across a film cut from one recording shows its frames at once. Opening a film of hundreds of clips no longer holds Studio while its captions and sounds are read, and the timeline no longer works through every clip again as the playhead moves.
