Export the timeline shown when you click Export, even if you keep editing while the job queues or runs. The export dialog loads its settings and progress without scanning the project folder. A trimmed clip outside a selected range can stay offline. Video exports mix audio while drawing the picture, prepare pages as needed, and fall back sooner when a hardware encoder stalls. On Apple silicon, Auto can use more drawing workers when enough memory is available. PNG stills use the browser's image directly when it already has the requested dimensions, and editing-project exports probe media in parallel. Closing the window on Windows minimizes it while an export is running.

The export queue shows each stage and its elapsed time, including audio mixing, loudness adjustment, muxing and saving. Percentages appear when work in that stage can be measured.
