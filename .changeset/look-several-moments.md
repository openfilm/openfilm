"openfilm": patch
---

`openfilm look` takes several times and ranges at once (`look 8.4-8.8 26.5 35.9-36.3`) and draws them in one sheet, opening the film once, so an agent checks every cut of a film, or picks through long footage, in one look instead of one look per frame. Before, only the last time or range given was drawn. A video of 30 s or more with sound and no transcript beside it says the one command that gives its words (`openfilm get asr --src …`), so what is said is read there instead of from frame after frame. The film and color skills no longer tell an agent to look at every shot or clip after each change: it looks as closely as the work needs, one moment or clip, several in one look, or footage that speaks by its words first.
