An agent the app runs reaches Studio with a key of its own: it reads the person's services and makes media with them, but connecting or disconnecting a service, its keys, the choices in Settings, Studio's preferences, and which plugins and skills are on, installed or let write are refused to it (only the person changes them, in Studio). When `openfilm get` makes something with a service or model other than the person's choice in Settings, its receipt says so (`madeWith`), and the media skill asks the agent to use the person's choice unless they ask otherwise, and to say which model it used.
