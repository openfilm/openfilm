A filter put in with a double click or "+" goes on a track of its own from the playhead, a clip that grades every layer under it while it is on (its strength in the inspector); dropped on a clip, it grades that clip alone. Clicking a filter tries it on the whole picture. Filters, effects, transitions and templates show on square cards; a filter's card is the film's own picture at the playhead in that filter, an effect's card its page laid over that picture. Filter and effect clips have colors and marks of their own on the timeline.
