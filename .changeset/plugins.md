Settings has a Plugins page: OpenFilm's workbenches (Edit, Canvas, Motion) are plugins you turn on or off there, in
place of "Ways of working" under Advanced. A plugin's workbench shows, and your agent is given the skills that come
with it, only while it is on: with Canvas off, agents are not offered the canvas skill and the manual no longer names
it. One workbench always stays on, and a film made with a plugin plays and exports the same with it off. The choice
is now kept for the desktop app, Studio in a browser and the CLI together; what you had chosen before is carried over.
