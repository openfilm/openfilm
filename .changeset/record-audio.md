Studio: record sound from the microphone on the timeline. The microphone button on the timeline's toolbar starts recording; while it records, the toolbar shows the time recorded, a level meter, Stop and Discard. Stop saves the recording as a WAV file in the project's assets, where it shows in the media, and puts it on a sound track where the playhead was when recording began, so it can be used at once. Discard keeps nothing. A microphone that is not allowed or not there is said in one sentence. The desktop app on macOS may use the microphone and says why when it first asks.
