`openfilm edit add <file…>` puts files of the project in the film: each a clip at the end of the track its kind goes on (a video or a picture on the last picture track, a sound on the last sound track, a new track when there is none), one after the other, and prints each new clip as a link, `film.html#<id>`. It is how the edit takes what an agent or another plugin hands it: the sender names the files, and where they go is the edit's to say. OpenFilm's own plugins bring their commands the way anyone's do, from a `package.json` in their folder.
