Studio: Shift-click adds a layer to what is selected, or takes it away, both on the picture and in the Layers panel; ⌘-click / Ctrl-click on a Layers row does the same. Shift with a box drawn on the picture adds the layers in it. The Layers panel now shows every selected layer as selected, and a Shift-click on the one layer selected, inside its own box, takes it away instead of starting to move it. Two Shift-clicks in a row no longer start typing into a line of text.
