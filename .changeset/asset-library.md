The assets drawer has a library: its categories along the top (Media, Audio, Voices, Text, Stickers, Effects, Transitions, Filters) and each one's groups down the left, OpenFilm's own transitions, effects, filters, text, stickers and sounds free to use in any film. Point at a thing and it plays; click it to try it on the picture in the viewer; double-click it, press Enter or click its + to put it in at the playhead (a transition on the cut, a filter on the picked clips, else the clip under the playhead), then change it in the inspector. Drag a filter onto a clip, or a dissolve onto a cut. Star what you use often: it is in Favorites in every project.

Voices lists the ready-made voices of ElevenLabs (Eleven v4), Inworld (TTS-2) and Cartesia (Sonic 3.6), as each service lists them, by service, language and gender. Click one to hear its sample; double-click it, press Enter or click its + to put it in the chat, and your agent makes the voice-over in it. A voice whose service is not connected yet says where to connect it.

Media in the library is what came into the project, folded under Imported: its own media, and what it took from the library; then Saved. Sounds show their waveform across the row, lit as they play. One highlight color, the timeline's teal, in every pane.
