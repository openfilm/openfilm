The assets drawer has a library: its categories along the top (Media, Audio, Text, Stickers, Effects, Transitions, Filters) and each one's groups down the left, OpenFilm's own transitions, effects, filters, text, stickers and sounds free to use in any film. Point at a thing and it plays; click it to try it on the picture in the viewer; double-click it, press Enter or click its + to put it in at the playhead (a transition on the cut, a filter on the picked clips, else the clip under the playhead), then change it in the inspector. Drag a filter onto a clip, or a dissolve onto a cut. Star what you use often: it is in Favorites in every project.
