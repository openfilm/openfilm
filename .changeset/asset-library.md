The assets drawer has a library: down its left, Media (the project's and Saved, as before), then Audio, Text, Stickers, Effects, Transitions and Filters that come with OpenFilm, free to use in any film. A thing plays as you point at it; click it to see what it becomes in the film, drag it onto the timeline, or add it at the playhead. A transition dropped near a cut goes over the cut, its middle on it; a filter goes on the clips you picked; a dissolve or a dip on the nearest cut.
