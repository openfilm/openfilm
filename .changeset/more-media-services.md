More media services for `openfilm get` and Studio, each with your own key: Pexels and Pixabay find pictures on the web (`image-search`), Brave Search and Tavily search the web and read pages (`web-search`, `web-fetch`; with Brave, pages are read from your machine), fal.ai now makes music (Stable Audio 2.5, Lyria 2) and sound effects (ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2, Stable Audio 2.5), and Groq transcribes fast with Whisper and speaks with Orpheus. Keys can also come from `PEXELS_API_KEY`, `PIXABAY_API_KEY`, `BRAVE_API_KEY`, `TAVILY_API_KEY` and `GROQ_API_KEY`.
