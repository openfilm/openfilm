`openfilm get video --resolution` asks for a clip's size, 480p to 4K, as the chosen model makes them: Seedance 2.0 up to 4K, Wan up to 1080p, HeyGen up to 2K. Without it a clip is 720p, or the model's nearest; a size the model does not make is its nearest, and the run says so.
