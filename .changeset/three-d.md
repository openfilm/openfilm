Pictures in 3D: a clip that sets or animates `--fx-z` (toward the viewer), `--fx-rx` or `--fx-ry` (its turn about its X and Y axes) is placed so about its center, under the film's camera (a perspective as a 50 mm lens on the stage), which the film has once any picture of it is in 3D; a flat film is placed as it always was (SPEC §1).

In Studio's Motion, a layer's 3D switch (the cube) adds its Z position and X and Y rotation to its Transform, each with a stopwatch, a value in blue and keyframes. A video export can blur what moves as a camera's shutter does (motion blur, 90° to 360°): each frame the average of eight moments.
