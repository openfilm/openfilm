A library page for what is yours beyond any one project, with room to tend it: Mine, in the folders you make, with a
file picked played beside it, and Storage, where projects are kept and the room they and their caches take. Open it
from Settings → General → Storage ("Manage…") or from "Manage…" under Mine in the assets drawer. In the desktop app it
is a tab of its own beside your projects' tabs; in a browser it is Studio's `/library` page.

Dropping files into Mine in the desktop app no longer leaves a card at 0% behind each one.
