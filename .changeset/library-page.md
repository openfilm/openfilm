Asset management: one page for everything of yours beyond any one project, opened from Settings → General. Projects
(the projects window, filling the page), Mine (your own media in the folders you make, a file picked played beside it
with what it is and where it is), Favorites (what you starred in OpenFilm's library, every category together, sounds
playable, unstarred in place), Skills (installed, starred, to discover) and Storage (where projects are kept, the room
they and their caches take). In the desktop app it is a tab of its own beside your projects' tabs, with its own icon;
in a browser it is Studio's `/library` page. Settings now has this one entry in place of its storage rows.

Dropping files into Mine in the desktop app no longer leaves a card at 0% behind each one.
