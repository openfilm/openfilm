The Community in the desktop app's sidebar is laid out as a page of its own. Its first page has the week's picks, then its places as chips: Saved, Tutorials, Example films, Skills, Plugins, Assets, Creators and the model leaderboard. Skills, Plugins and Assets open a menu of their categories (plugins are editors or connectors; templates, music and sound effects are assets). A place opens with its name large and its categories under it, and the way back and forward remembers the category. The model leaderboard ranks the top 100 models on Arena's WebDev board, with what each task costs. Publish sits at the top right.
