Studio: a text color typed into its field, then a stroke added with the next click, keeps the color. Leaving the field and clicking Add stroke made the two edits at once, and the stroke was worked out from the film as it was before the color, so writing it took the color away again: the words went back to the color they had. The same held for any two changes to a layer made that close together.
