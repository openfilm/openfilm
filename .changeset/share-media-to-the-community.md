You can share with the Community straight from Studio, with your OpenFilm account and no git. A maintainer of the Community looks at everything before it shows there.

Media: right-click a video, a picture or a sound in your library, or a whole folder, and choose Share to the Community. Pick the name you share under the first time, say what it is and where it comes from, and Studio checks the files, draws each video's poster and uploads them. Several files go as one pack.

A film: from a project's menu in Projects, or from the Community window's Publish page, share the film you made as an example anyone opens as a project of their own. You choose what of it goes: the edit alone (the film and every file it plays), or its canvas and the rest of its media too. Its history, your conversations with the agent and its logs are never shared, and a file that holds what looks like a key stops the share. Films people shared show among the Community's examples, under their names.

The Publish page lists what you shared with how each review went, what the maintainer said when one was not accepted, and a way to take one back. Your own page in the Community shows who you are there, and a pack's page, a shared film's page and an author's page have a Report link.
