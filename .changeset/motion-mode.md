One button on Studio's top bar says which of Edit, Canvas and Motion the panes are for, and opens a menu of the three. Motion shows the film as an animation editor lays out a composition, in the timeline's place (the picture and the inspector stay): every picture clip a layer, the topmost first, its bar over its time; ▸ twirls a layer open to its position, scale, rotation and opacity, each a row of keyframes or the graph. P, S, R and T show only that property of the picked layers (⇧ adds), U the ones that move; J and K go to the keyframe before and after the playhead. Keyframes picked on a layer pick it first, as on the timeline. Its panel is laid out as an animation editor's timeline: the current time big at the top left; each layer's label color, number, name and blending mode; its bar dragged to move it and by its ends to trim it; under its Transform each property with the keyframe navigator, a stopwatch and its value in blue, dragged left and right or clicked and typed; a zoom slider under the layers; the work area (the marked range) over the ruler.
