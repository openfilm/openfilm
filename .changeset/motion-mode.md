One button on Studio's top bar says which of Edit, Canvas and Motion the panes are for, and opens a menu of the three. Motion shows the film as an animation editor lays out a composition, in the timeline's place (the picture and the inspector stay): every picture clip a layer, the topmost first, its bar over its time; ▸ twirls a layer open to its position, scale, rotation and opacity, each a row of keyframes or the graph. P, S, R and T show only that property of the picked layers (⇧ adds), U the ones that move; J and K go to the keyframe before and after the playhead. Keyframes picked on a layer pick it first, as on the timeline.
