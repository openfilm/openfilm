Connectors: your agent can reach the services you work in. Six come with OpenFilm, in the Community's Plugins under Connectors: Dropbox (find and fetch your files), Frame.io (review comments, upload versions, fetch media), Notion (scripts and shot lists from your pages, and back), Adobe (Express, Firefly, Photoshop and Adobe Stock tools), Canva (find and make designs, such as covers) and Vimeo (upload films, edit their details, read their analytics; beta). Get and connect one and its service's sign-in page opens in your browser; sign in once and every agent in the app can use it, OpenFilm's own as well as Claude Code and Codex, with no more sign-ins. Its page says whether it is connected, offers things to say (a click puts one in the chat), how it works and what it can do, reading apart from changing; before any agent changes anything in a service, OpenFilm asks you (allow once, or while OpenFilm is open), whatever that agent's own permissions; a tool call shows in the chat by its service ("Notion · search"). What you signed in with is kept sealed in your system's keychain and refreshed by the app, never handed to an agent: each reaches the service through the app. Disconnect, turn off or uninstall from its page or from Mine. A plugin is a connector when its folder has a `.mcp.json` as Claude Code plugins have (`{ "mcpServers": { "<name>": { "type": "http", "url": "https://…" } } }`).
