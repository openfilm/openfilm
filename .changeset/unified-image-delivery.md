Studio: Images and Slides & PDF are one export page, with four delivery types: individual image files, ZIP, PDF and PPTX. All four keep the same selected frames and framing. Image files are saved separately; ZIP stays a ZIP even for a single frame. Existing saved settings and frame selections are carried over. The export dialog is a little smaller, still adapting to the window, with its picture preview kept within the available space.
