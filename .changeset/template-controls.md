A template's places can be framed: pick one in the inspector to replace what is in it, fill the picture or fit it whole, zoom in, move the point looked at, and for a video choose where it starts. Its colors change all at once in a scheme (Original, Warm, Cool, Black and white, Neon, Soft), and a font it offers is picked from the fonts. Made longer or shorter, from Length in the inspector or by pulling its clip's edge on the timeline, its places stretch while its opening and its end keep their timing. Another template can take its place from the inspector, your clips and the words you wrote moving into it. Each is one step, undone as any other.
