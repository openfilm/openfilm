A new picture node on the canvas is square (and made square until another shape is picked), named "Picture 1" and renamed with a double click on its name; empty, it shows only its mark on a soft light. Its composer is laid out like a text node's: what it is made from shows as rounded tiles left of a "+" (upload, from the canvas, from the Assets), each pressed to show it or find it on the board, with a × off its corner; a tools button in its corner opens a row of a character, a style and a preset (a clip: a character and an effect). The model picker shows each model with its maker's mark (ByteDance, OpenAI, Google, Kling, MiniMax, Alibaba, Black Forest Labs, xAI), whatever service it is reached through, and the shape, quality and count read as one quiet line.
