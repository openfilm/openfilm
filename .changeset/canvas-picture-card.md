A new picture node on the canvas is square (and made square until another shape is picked), named "Picture 1" and renamed with a double click on its name; empty, it shows only its mark on a soft light. Its composer is laid out like a text node's: what it is made from shows as rounded tiles left of a "+" (upload, from the canvas, from the Assets), each pressed to show it or find it on the board, with a × off its corner; a tools button in its corner opens a row of a character, a style and a preset (a clip: a character and an effect). The model button shows its service's mark, the models' list no longer shows how long a model took, and the shape, quality and count read as one quiet line. The bar over a picture or clip node matches a text node's, starts with "Ask in chat", and shows on an empty one too (with Upload).

The presets are pictures now: a picture node's presets open as cards, each a picture of what it makes, a kind at a time (story, characters, places, products, design, retouch), with those that need a picture wired in marked. Fourteen are new, from what the picture models' guides show people make most: an outfit change, an isometric diorama, concept art, a white-background product shot, an ad shot, an exploded view, a movie poster, a sticker sheet, an infographic, a video thumbnail, an old photo restored, line art colored, a picture made real, and a new background.
