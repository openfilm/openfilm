Export editing projects for Final Cut Pro as FCPXML with their media, the main picture track on the primary storyline, connected overlays and separate audio clips or mixed stems. Separate audio clips retain Studio's row grouping with an audio role per row. HTML pages render as ProRes clips with alpha; subtitles are included as editable title clips above the picture tracks with a separate SRT copy.
