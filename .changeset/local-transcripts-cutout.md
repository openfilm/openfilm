Local services transcribe and take backgrounds out on this computer, free and offline. Whisper Small or Whisper Large v3 Turbo writes down what a recording says, each word timed (each character, in Chinese and Japanese), in the language it hears; BiRefNet Lite makes a picture's background transparent, with `openfilm get cutout`. Installed, a model makes its kind when nothing else was chosen for it, and appears among that kind's choices in Settings → Services.
