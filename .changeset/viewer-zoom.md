Studio: the picture in the viewer zooms and pans. Pinch on a trackpad, or hold ⌘ (Ctrl on Windows) and turn the mouse wheel, to zoom about the pointer; while it is zoomed, scroll (Shift + wheel: sideways) or drag with the middle button to pan. The zoom shows in the viewer's bar, and pressing it fits the picture again. Selecting, moving and resizing on the picture work at any zoom, with handles the same size on screen. Once a zoom has settled, the film is drawn again behind the picture for its new size, so canvas and 3D pages are as sharp as they are seen, past the film's own size up to 64 megapixels a page; a drag, words typed in place or a crop meanwhile carry on, and the new drawing is shown when they end, with what they kept. When the film is drawn again (after a zoom or a resized viewer), the picture no longer flashes blank. The zoom changes only the viewer, never the film or its exports.
