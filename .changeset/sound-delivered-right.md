A film's sound is right by default. Export brings it to -14 LUFS (online video) unless you pick another loudness, and
a sound export of the mix can be brought to a loudness too. Every render and export catches the peaks of sounds that
add up past full scale, so a film never clips; stems stay as they are. The preview plays the film at the loudness it
is exported at, through the same kind of limiter, and the meters show what will be delivered.
