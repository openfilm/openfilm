Pictures from OpenAI are made with GPT Image 2.6 Flare by default, or 2.6 Sunburst; GPT Image 2.5 Flare and Sunburst stay, and GPT Image 2 is gone (a film set to it uses 2.6 Flare). `openfilm get image` takes `--markup`, a transparent PNG drawn on to say what of the first picture to change; `--format gif`, a looping animation of `--frames` (2 to 16) of `--frame-ms` each, its frames as large as GPT Image makes them in the shape asked; and `--format svg`, a vector picture from the prompt alone, its cost worked out with the writing's. A GIF, or an animated PNG or WebP, in a film now plays by the film's time: the frame its own timing has at the clip's time, from the clip's start, looping as the file says, the same in Studio, `look` and an export.
