Dragging the playhead no longer shows a black picture. A film whose picture is its pages (ten scenes, one at a time) opened a scene only as the playhead came to it, and a scene takes seconds to open: a drag across the film showed nothing for two thirds of it, a slow drag inside a scene not open yet showed nothing at all, and a click on another scene waited up to four seconds for its picture. While the film stands, it now opens the pages it may keep, nearest first, and draws each once out of sight; a page the drag reaches before then begins to open at that frame, and the picture that was there stays until it is in. A scene made of pages of its own is weighed by what those hold, so a film of ten such scenes keeps them all open.
