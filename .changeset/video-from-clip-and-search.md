`openfilm get video --video <clip>` makes a clip from another in the project, the model's way, chosen with `--model`: at fal, Kling O3 Edit changes what the prompt names and keeps the rest, Kling O3 Reference makes a new clip with its camera and motion, Seedance 2.0 takes it as a reference, Luma Ray 3.2 redraws it with its performance kept, and MiniMax H3 Max Recast puts the people of `--input-reference` in place of its own. The clip goes to fal's storage (a small one inline when that cannot be done), and the cost Studio asks about is for its length. `openfilm get video-search` finds video clips on the web at Pexels or Pixabay (1 to 4, `--orientation`), each downloaded near Full HD with its length, size and credit; Settings → Services lists it under video generation, with an icon of its own; the kinds that make media are named for it there (music generation, sound effect generation, image generation, video generation).
