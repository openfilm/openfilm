The pipeline works its stages: `openfilm pipeline <stage>` says how each one's work is done (the storyboard stage shows the form its file takes — one `## Shot n — seconds` entry with its page, voice, words on screen and media), `openfilm pipeline check` holds the storyboard against the project — pages written and cut into film.html, media present, voices recorded at `assets/audio/vo/shot-<n>.wav`, the seconds adding up — naming what is missing under the stage that fills it, and `openfilm pipeline` points at the current stage's guide.
