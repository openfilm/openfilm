A film can be a clip of another: `<iframe src="parts/intro/film.html">`, a film.html in a folder of its own. Its picture shows as a page's, and its sound is heard in the film that holds it, each sound where the clip plays that part of it, at its volume times the clip's, the clip's fades on the ones at its edges. Exports and Studio's preview hear it too.

In Studio, ⌥G (or New compound clip in a clip's menu) makes the picked clips one compound clip: their own film in `parts/<name>/film.html`, one clip where they were, undone like any edit. ⌥⇧G (Break apart compound clip) puts its clips back where it plays them, cut to the part it plays. A double-click opens a compound clip's film to edit, with the project's media, and the timeline's toolbar leads back (‹ project / name); the film around it shows the change at once.
