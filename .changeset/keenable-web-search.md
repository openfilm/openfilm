Keenable for web search and reading pages, under More in Settings › Providers, beside Brave Search and Tavily. It works without a key, so Connect connects it at once; a key added afterwards lifts its limits. Like every provider it serves nothing until it is connected, and the ? beside "Web search" in its row says that the searches and the addresses read go to Keenable.
