The preview keeps more of a film's pages open when they are light. It kept eight, whatever they were, because a browser gives a film only so many 3D contexts; a page of words, shapes and pictures holds none, so up to twenty-four of those now stay open once shown (pages in 3D, with their own footage or not opened yet count as before), and a jump or a drag back to one draws at once instead of loading its page again. Studio's preview also learns how long a video's frames last again: it asked for each file by an address its own server could not find, so a still frame that never reached the screen was not sought again there.
