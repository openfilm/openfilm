A text style or motion tried from the library shows the words a new line says, in the app's language, at the size and place it gets when put in, and a sticker tried shows at its put-in size too. A line of words breaks only where a new line is typed: one wider than its box is made smaller, never wrapped. Text motion cards draw their words and play the motion under the pointer; moving stickers fill their cards.
