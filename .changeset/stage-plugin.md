A new plugin, Stage (off until turned on, in beta): shots blocked out in 3D and played live. An agent writes a shot as a three.js page in `stage/<name>/`: a white-box set, figures that walk, run, sit, turn, look and point, glTF models and rigged characters, and a camera move. `openfilm stage look` shows it to the agent (frames, a view from above with every path, where each figure and the camera are, and what is wrong: the camera inside something, a figure walking through something, out of frame, hidden or cut by the frame). `openfilm stage render` makes the white-box video, its depth and poses (OpenPose) and its first frame, into `assets/stage/<name>/`; with Blender on the computer, `--engine blender` draws it with EEVEE or Cycles from the same page, and `openfilm stage blend` gives the scene as a .blend of your own. The Stage workbench shows the shot live, the view from above and the shots; dragging a figure or the camera puts a reference in the chat for the agent, and Make video lays the white box out on the canvas for a video model. A shot is a page of the film like any other, so it plays and renders with the plugin off.
