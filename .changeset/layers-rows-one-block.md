Studio: in the Layers panel, ⌘-click (Ctrl-click) adds a layer to the selection or takes it away, and Shift-click selects every row shown from the last one clicked to it (with ⌘ or Ctrl held too, it keeps what was selected). Rows selected one after another now join into one muted blue block, rounded only at its ends, and the layers inside a selected group are tinted with it; a row under the pointer is lined. A box drawn on the picture is filled more lightly.
