The library's skills fold by part (yours, system, community, each agent's own), and any skill can be starred into the side bar's Favorites. A skill to discover shows a star and a "+" over its cover: the "+" names it in the chat's message, and Install is on its page. In the desktop app the chat's composer over the library looks as it does over the chat, without a shadow darkening the library around it.
