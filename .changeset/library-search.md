Agents search OpenFilm's own library before they make anything, as part of `openfilm get`: `get music-search` and `get sfx-search` beside `get music` and `get sfx`, and `get transition`, `effect`, `filter`, `sticker`, `text` and `template`, each `--prompt "<what>"` in any words and language, free and offline, and `--take <id>` to copy one into the project with what to write in film.html. `openfilm get` lists them first. The library's music and sounds now carry their mood, instruments, use, energy, tempo and whether they loop; its other things say what they look like and when to use them.
