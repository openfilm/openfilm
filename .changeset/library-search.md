Agents search OpenFilm's own library before they make anything: `openfilm find <kind> "<what>"` finds music, sound effects, transitions, effects, filters, stickers, text and templates by what they are for, in any language and offline, and `openfilm take <id>` copies one into the project with what to write in film.html. The library's music and sounds now carry their mood, instruments, use, energy, tempo and whether they loop; its other things say what they look like and when to use them.
