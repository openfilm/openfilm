Studio: smooth selected audio cuts with 5, 10 or 20 ms fades, or enable automatic smoothing for new cuts in a project. Studio's edit API applies the policy and includes its fades in the edit's undo step. Outside file writes are checked with an explicit repair action. Continuous source audio, existing fades, locked or muted tracks and opted-out clips are left alone; clip timing and linked pictures stay as they are.
