An editor's help is a dialog in the middle of the window, from the "?" in the editor menu or the ? key anywhere (not while typing): what its plugin says of it, then what the editor shows itself (Screening: how it is used step by step and its keys), then the keys between editors. Plugins add theirs with `help.set(editor, draw)`. Screening no longer has a help button of its own nor the sheet shown while ⌘ is held, and its speed and full-screen buttons look like the edit's. The cover's tile beside the main track is one size always, as tall as the main track at its lowest, and the main track starts a little taller. A new project's conversation opens on the question and a line of what to do, above the message box, with cards for the library and the Community.
