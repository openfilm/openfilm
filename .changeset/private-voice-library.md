Add My voices to the resource library: make a voice from a recording with OpenFilm's service, with Inworld (recommended) or ElevenLabs as its engine. The voice is its recording, kept on your computer and sent with each line spoken in it, so it keeps working when you sign in to OpenFilm again; removing it also deletes its clone at the engine. Simplify the voice navigation to Favorites, My voices and Providers.
