Studio: the library's Captions are one page. It says how much is said in the film, which clips have no transcript yet, and which caption track the captions are on. Making them again puts what is said now, after the cuts, in place of that track's captions, their look kept. Clips that cannot be transcribed (no service) no longer stop the rest from being made captions. A subtitle file (SRT or WebVTT) dropped on the timeline or the picture becomes a caption track from where it lands, and "Add a caption" puts one at the playhead on the top caption track. Words with their times keep the line's own spaces ("Comfortable, right?").
