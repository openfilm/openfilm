Plugins are now a tab of the library, beside Skills, instead of a page in Settings. A plugin is one thing OpenFilm can do, for your agent and for you alike, under one switch: Edit (the film, its picture, its timeline), Canvas and Motion. On, its workbench is shown and your agent is given its skill; off, neither. Every plugin can be off at once: a project then shows only its library, and your agent is given none of their skills. Your media is not a plugin: it is always there. OpenFilm's own system skills are found on their plugin's page and no longer have a switch of their own or a place among your Skills. Sound, Color and Compositing are listed as in development. Each plugin is a folder with its own description, so one more is one more folder. A plugin's workbench is a whole surface of its own, shown in place of the edit and the canvas: with more than those two on, the button on the top bar opens a menu of them.
