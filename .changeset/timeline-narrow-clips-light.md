The editor stays quick with a long film zoomed to fit. A clip a few pixels wide shows nothing but its color, yet was drawn with everything a wide one has (its name, its bands, each a layer of its own): a film of fifteen hundred clips was ten thousand elements in the timeline, and every move of the pointer, anywhere in Studio, looked through them all. A narrow clip is now one element, drawn whole again as soon as it is selected or in hand; it is picked and trimmed as before.
