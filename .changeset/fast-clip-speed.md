Studio: a video or sound clip's speed goes from 0.1× to 100× (it was 0.25× to 4×), its sound's pitch kept in exports. The inspector sets it on a slider marked 0.1×, 1×, 2×, 5×, 10× and 100×, with the number beside it, the clip's new length shown as you drag, and a reset (or a double-click) back to 1×. Dragging a clip's edge with Speed on (in the clip's right-click menu) reaches the same range. In the preview, a clip the browser can't play that fast or that slow (past 16× or below 1/16×, its speed by the viewer's) shows its picture frame by frame, without its sound; the export has both. A film with a speed outside 0.25×–4× doesn't open in OpenFilm 0.2.6 or older.
