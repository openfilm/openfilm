Studio: the library's Captions are New captions and Caption templates. New captions makes a caption track from the clips you choose on the timeline. A clip with a transcript, as a voice-over has, is used as it is, and only those marked "To transcribe" are heard first. A subtitle file works too, and either can be translated into a language on a track just above it. Caption templates puts a caption look on every caption of a track, and also on the captions given a look of their own when you ask. A clip whose words the captions come from shows a captions mark before its name on the timeline.
