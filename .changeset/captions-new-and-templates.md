Studio: the library's New captions makes caption tracks of what the film says or of a subtitle file. A sound that already has its words, as a voice-over has, is used as it is, with nothing to choose. Only the sounds that need recognizing are ticked, in the list or at a clip's top right on the timeline, where the clips the captions are made of are framed while the page is open. What to make is ticked too: the captions in the words said, their translation into a language on a track of its own, or the translation alone. A line under the button says what will happen. A clip whose words the captions come from shows a CC mark before its name on the timeline.
