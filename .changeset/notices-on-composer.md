The chat's notices are one kind of card on the composer, rising in, each with its mark (information, warning, error), its words, what to do about it and a close button where it can be left: an agent that cannot be used any more, a turn that failed (with Copy diagnostics, Switch account), a balance run out, a message waiting for its turn, the context almost full, and Studio's own trouble (ffmpeg, its browser), which was a strip over the chat. Undoing a turn is asked on the composer too, not in a dialog over the window. Studio's installed skills are listed in this order: All, Imported, System, Community, Agents' own.
