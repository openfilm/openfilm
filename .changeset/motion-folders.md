Motion's rows follow the edit's tracks: a track of one picture clip is that layer, a track of several is a folder
showing its clips on one row, twirled open to a layer each. A page opened in Motion lists its top elements, each with
the elements inside it twirled open on demand; its playhead stays on the clip, and its layers stay listed when the
playhead is off the picture.
