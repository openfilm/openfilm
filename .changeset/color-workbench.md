The color workbench (beta, off until it is turned on in Plugins): each video clip graded by hand, as a colorist grades it. Its picture is the clip's footage read at its own depth and graded by the same LUT the film plays and exports through, beside the waveform, RGB parade, vectorscope and histogram. Camera log from thirteen makers' published curves (Apple Log and Log 2, S-Log3, LogC3 and LogC4, Canon Log 3, V-Log, F-Log and F-Log2, N-Log, D-Log, Blackmagic Film Gen 5), HLG and PQ, or a camera maker's LUT; exposure and white balance in kelvin and tint; lift, gamma, gain and offset wheels; contrast, highlights, shadows, saturation and vibrance; curves; hue and saturation curves; windows that change one range of colors, checked on their matte; a look LUT of your own, mixed in. White balance from a click on something grey, automatic balance, matching one clip to another, copying a grade, undo. A clip's grade is a LUT of the project named in its `--of-lut`, so the film plays the same with the plugin off; `openfilm color` (`show`, `set`, `auto`, `match`, `copy`, `reset`, `look`) changes the same grades, and `look` measures a clip as it plays and draws it with its scopes.
