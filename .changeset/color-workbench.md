The color workbench (beta, off until it is turned on in Plugins) is laid out as a grading page: the edit's picture with scopes beside it (waveform, RGB parade or histogram, a vectorscope with its targets and skin-tone line, and the middle in IRE, the white in kelvin and what is clipped, measured on the footage at its own depth as it is exported), a strip of the film's shots as they play graded, and a band of tools under them. On the left, a shot's grade as it applies, top to bottom: the footage's input, the layers it shares with other shots, its own, a filter or LUT for the screen, the output; the one picked is what the tools change. In the middle, basic sliders, shadows, midtones, highlights and overall wheels (each with its red, green and blue, and a reset), RGB curves, hue curves, and the library's filters shown on the shot; a white picker makes a spot clicked on the picture grey through the grade, and each page can be reset on its own. On the right, automatic balance, matching to a shot picked in the strip, copying a grade to shots, reset and export as a .cube LUT. Under the picture, the grade, a split with the footage on the left of a line, or the footage alone (⇧D). ← and → go from shot to shot in the strip, ⌘C and ⌘V copy a grade. Every change is the project's color files, one step of the film's undo, and an agent's change to them shows in the workbench at once: the shots and layers it changed light up, and a list of changes says who changed what. With a chat, shots and their color files can be handed to the agent.
