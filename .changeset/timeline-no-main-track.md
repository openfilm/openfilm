The timeline no longer has a main track, a main-track magnet or linkage. Nothing moves unless you ask: Delete leaves a gap, a dragged or pasted clip covers what is under it (hold Option while dragging to insert), and Option-arrow nudges a clip over what is there. Shift-Delete, closing a gap, Q / W, inserts and Duplicate ripple every unlocked track together: what starts after the edit point moves, what starts before it (a music bed) stays, and another track moves only as far as it has room. The toolbar keeps the snapping switch.
