Studio: the library drawer's side bar is namespaces, boxes as compact as an editor's: a box picks, or opens to the lines it holds, and stays as it was left. The media's are Import (this project) and Mine (the person's folders, those of the library folder, made there or linked from another disk or a NAS; one alone is Mine itself, more open under it, in the order the person drags). The media's "+ Add" makes or links a folder, gets media from the Community, says how to make media and contributes them (each folder uploads in its own pane). Every other category starts with Favorites, the things starred in it (a star on each card and row), then Official (OpenFilm's groups) and Community (packs added), the text's word art and templates; "+ Add" at its foot opens upward with getting from the Community, how to make things, contributing them and putting back a pack taken out. The voice-over's are Mine, Favorites and Services (all, then each service); Mine has tabs on the right: the person's voices, Record and Clone, a voice cloned in place. Files dropped on a folder go in it: from this computer, from another folder, from the project, or a thing of a pack copied there. The "+" at the side bar's foot uploads files, makes or links a folder, gets more from the Community, says how to make things and contributes them; a pack is taken out of the library from its menu and put back from "+". The projects window has a side bar: the OpenFilm account at its top (signed in, its picture and name; else the local account and Sign in), then Projects and Examples, and Start with Edit or Canvas (a new project opened on that workbench); at its foot the Community's news and this version, with what is new in it and the update when one is ready. Projects open on New project, Open folder and Get from GitHub side by side, then all projects under a bar that stays as they scroll, its tools (search, new folder, sort, view, more, the Recycle Bin) at its end. Settings › General has Storage as one row, what OpenFilm takes and what is free, opening its own page. In OpenFilm's own Recycle Bin, what is deleted in OpenFilm (files, folders, projects, skills, voices, subjects) can be restored, deleted permanently or emptied; something on another disk still goes to that disk's system trash. Save to puts clips and files into one of the person's folders. The first time, what lay loose at the library's top moves into the asset library. In the chat, "+" opens a compact menu lined up with it, with files or photos (⌘U), a folder, skills and references; `/` names a skill and `@` points at things, and the Community button is back beside the library's. An empty conversation asks what to make, with a line under it for each of the two buttons at the message box's right, with their marks: the Community (plugins, skills and material to add) and the library (media to import, templates, music and effects), each opening it.
