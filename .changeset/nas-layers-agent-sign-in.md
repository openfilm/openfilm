Link Windows media files and folders even when a network share refuses system links. The project's references keep the originals in place and work for preview and export. Use Alt to toggle whole clips and their layers together in the Layers panel, or Shift to select a range by clicking or dragging. OpenFilm's agent asks for sign-in when its credential is rejected and keeps technical service errors in its connection details.
