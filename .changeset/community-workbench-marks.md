The Community is laid out as a store of extensions. Its side, under a plain Search: Mine, then the Community's Plugins, Skills, Library and Examples, and at its foot Publish and Add (import a folder or a link, or another catalog to read).

Everything is got before it is yours, then managed. A card in the store says Get, or Installed, or Off. A page opens with its icon large, its name, who made it and what it is for, then what to do: Get; once got, Turn off or Turn on, and Uninstall (a skill also Try now and Update); its facts (kind, maker, users, version, scripts, source) at its side, then its preview, what it does and how to use it. OpenFilm's built-in plugins (Screening, Edit, Canvas) can be turned off, not uninstalled. Mine lists only what you got: your plugins in rows you drag (or move with Option and an arrow) into your own order, which the top right's switch and its shortcuts follow, your skills, and your packs, each managed from its gear (turn off, see details, uninstall); an agent's own skills and connections are folded in a line each.

Plugins are of two kinds, editors (a way of working on the film, picked at the top right) and connectors (a service your agent reaches). Skills are shown as cards with their cover, playing their preview under the pointer, a grid for each kind of work and a chip to see one kind. The Library shows each kind's packs by their own pictures, a chip to see one kind with all its things; a thing opens large over the page, stepped through with the arrows, with its pack, maker and licence. Packs are got whole and said Added; a pack the Community has a newer version of says so, and Mine updates it; packs can be removed from the library, and OpenFilm's own removed ones are put back from Mine. Media packs (footage, pictures, recordings) are saved as a folder of yours under Mine, named after the pack. Examples, whole projects made with OpenFilm, are a kind of the Community like any other, kept in their publishers' repositories and read from the Community's index with its news; one opened is copied into a new project from the projects window. Every plugin and skill has an app icon (a square icon.svg, icon.png or icon.webp at the root of its folder); one without gets the default plugin or skill icon.
