The Community is laid out as an app store. Its side, under a plain Search, with quiet line marks: Mine, then the Community's Plugins, Skills, Library and Examples, and at its foot Publish and Add (import a folder or a link, or another catalog to read). Mine is managed as Settings lists things: what can be updated first, then your plugins in rows you drag (or move with Option and an arrow) into your own order, which the top right's switch and its shortcuts follow, then your skills, each on its switch, and an agent's own skills and connections folded. Plugins, Skills and Library each open on a banner of what is put forward, then shelves: OpenFilm's and the Community's plugins, skills by the kind of work they help with, the library's kinds as coloured tiles and their packs, Media first (packs of footage, pictures and recordings, for the library's Media tab: adding a media pack fetches its files into a new folder under Mine, named after it, each file under its name; one file alone goes into the first folder); rows have a Get button, and the one thing to do with a pack or a thing is to add it to the library. Examples, whole projects made with OpenFilm, are a kind of the Community like any other: each kept in its publisher's own repository and listed in the Community's index, OpenFilm's own among them, and read from there with the Community's news. They have their own page too (a banner, then a shelf for each kind of film, and how to list one); one opened is its page in the projects window, where it is copied into a new project. A skill's page is a product page: its icon large beside its name, what it is for, who made it and Get or Try now; a strip of facts (how many use it, its kind of work, its maker and how far that is vouched for, its version, its scripts); then its preview, what it does, its profile and what it may do on this computer. Favorites are gone. Every plugin and skill has an app icon, made as an iOS app icon: a square icon.svg, icon.png or icon.webp at the root of its folder, drawn everywhere in the same continuous rounded square; one without gets the default plugin or skill icon. OpenFilm's own come with colourful icons. Workbench names drop the suffix (Screening, Edit, Canvas, Color, Sound, Motion, 3D) and come in that order, every one but Edit and Canvas marked beta. The top right's switch shows only the name; its menu shows each icon on a solid background.
