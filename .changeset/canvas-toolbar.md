The canvas's toolbar adds a node with one click: text, a picture, a sound, a clip or a cut, each in view and clear of the others, beside the libraries and upload. A small bar at the bottom left, matching the one at the bottom right, holds the keys (their panel opens in the middle, over the toolbar) and one button that switches between select and the hand; a switched-on button in either bar now has a clearly lighter ground. The menu a double click opens is larger and easier to read. An empty canvas says "Double-click the canvas to add a node", the toolbar's buttons sink and spring back when pressed, a new node comes in with a small pop, and a double click on the board leaves a ring where it was. The Beta tag is gone.
