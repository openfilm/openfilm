Anyone can make a plugin: a skill with a web page at its folder's root (`index.html`) is one. It is added as a skill is (a folder, a link, a project's own `skills/`), listed in the library's Plugins tab instead of among your Skills, and turned by one switch: on, your agent is given the skill and its page is a workbench you can switch to from the top bar, shown beside the library. The page is told where the project's files are (`?project=`) and reads them; it cannot reach the editor or change the project. Studio also remembers which plugin's workbench each project was left on.
