Plugins have a window of their own instead of a tab of the library: the puzzle beside the chat's "+", or Plugins in the OpenFilm menu, opens it. It shows each plugin and skill as a card, under the one category of what it helps make (material, editing, picture, sound, recipes, tools): its mark, its name, what it is for, and its switch. A click opens its page, which sums it up at the top: how hard it is to start with, how little work a result takes, what it costs, how it is used (its workbench, the conversation, commands) and who it is for, then what it does and where to open it. Installed and Discover are at the top of the window, with search and Import. A plugin or a skill says these in its openfilm.json (`category`, `difficulty`, `ease`, `audience`, `cost`, `details`; plugins/README.md). Screening, Motion and the stage have marks of their own.
