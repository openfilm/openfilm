The Plugins tab lists everything by what it helps make, each in one category (material, editing, picture, sound, recipes, tools), with chips to show one; a line says only its name, what it is for and where it comes from. A plugin's or a skill's page sums it up at the top: how hard it is to start with, how little work a result takes, what it costs, how it is used (its workbench, the conversation, commands) and who it is for, then what it does and where to open it. A plugin or a skill says these in its openfilm.json (`category`, `difficulty`, `ease`, `audience`, `cost`, `details`; plugins/README.md). Screening, Motion and the stage have marks of their own.
