OpenFilm has a Community: one window for everything people make and share, opened from the compass beside the chat's "+" or Community in the OpenFilm menu (the menu keeps OpenFilm's groups and accounts in its own flyout, Join us). It has three places: Discover, to look around (a featured pick, every kind at a glance, a shelf per kind, and where people talk); Added, what you have, by kind, with its switches (OpenFilm's workbenches, your skills, and the skills and connectors each agent brings, each let in or not); and Publish, how to share what you made. Kinds are listed once and the list is open: workbenches, skills, connectors (the MCP servers your agents bring), and the library's templates, text, stickers, effects, transitions, filters, sounds and music, with the library's own marks. A thing added goes where it is used: the top right, the conversation, your agent, the library's tabs. Cards show a picture, what it is for, its kind and its button (Install, Turn on, or said Installed, On or In the library); what is still being made shows Coming soon. Import, at the side's foot, takes a folder or a link and works out its kind. An item's page sums it up at the top (how hard it is to start with, how little work a result takes, what it costs, where you use it, who it is for), says what it is for and where to open it, and folds what it is made of under For developers. A plugin or a skill says these in its openfilm.json (`category`, `difficulty`, `ease`, `audience`, `cost`, `details`; plugins/README.md). Screening, Motion and the stage have marks of their own.

The large windows (Settings, Community, projects, export, version history) share one size and one look: the side on the left in the panels' color, the page on the right a step lighter in the dark (a step darker side in the light), and Esc pressed in one is its own.
