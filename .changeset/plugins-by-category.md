Plugins have a window of their own instead of a tab of the library: the puzzle beside the chat's "+", or Plugins in the OpenFilm menu, opens it. It is laid out as Settings is: a search, Installed, Discover and the categories (material, editing, picture, sound, recipes, tools) down the side, Import at its foot, and on the right each plugin and skill as a row with its mark, its name, what it is for and its switch. Discover keeps what you install, marked Installed. A click opens a page that sums it up at the top: how hard it is to start with, how little work a result takes, what it costs, how it is used (its workbench, the conversation, commands) and who it is for, then what it does and where to open it. A plugin or a skill says these in its openfilm.json (`category`, `difficulty`, `ease`, `audience`, `cost`, `details`; plugins/README.md). Screening, Motion and the stage have marks of their own.

The large windows (Settings, Plugins, projects, export, version history) share one size and one look: the side on the left in the panels' color, the page on the right a step lighter in the dark (a step darker side in the light), and Esc pressed in one is its own.
