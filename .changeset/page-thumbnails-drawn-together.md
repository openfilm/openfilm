A film's page clips get their first thumbnails in about half the time. The first time a project was opened, each of its pages was opened in the thumbnail browser up to three times: once to ask its length and size, once for its poster and once for its frames on the timeline, each at a width of its own, one page at a time while most of the machine stood idle. A page is now opened once for all three: a smaller picture is taken of the page open at the poster's width, and what the page says of itself is kept as its pictures are drawn. Up to three pages are drawn at once where the machine has the cores for it, and a page that is slow to come ready no longer holds the next one's picture.
