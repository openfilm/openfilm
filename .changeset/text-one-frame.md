A line of words is one frame on the picture, its box always its words: while it is typed in, its frame follows the words (a line hugs them, one pulled to a width keeps it and grows taller), the whole clip stays in hand when typing ends, and the words, box and size are one undo. New words or a new look from the inspector refit it the same way, and its words' size reads in the film's pixels.
