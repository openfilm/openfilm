In Studio's Motion, a clip twirled open has a Mask group as an animation editor's: its shape (the inspector's: linear, mirror, ellipse, rectangle, star, heart), inverted or not, and its position, size, turn, feather and corners each with a stopwatch, a value in blue and keyframes. A mask that moves is written a stop a frame (`@keyframes <clip>--mask`, its keyframes heading it as a comment), so every shape moves exactly as drawn; a copied clip gets its own.
