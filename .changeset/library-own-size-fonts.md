Every text template is put in at its own size: the full-picture ones (titles, lower thirds, badges, the countdown, the progress bar, the subtitle box) now draw in a box of their own, in the middle of the picture or near its foot. Every text template and every sticker with words has a Font property, so its font is changed with the font picker in the inspector, and a template's Text field shows its own words. The stickers are listed as Favorites, Animated stickers and Still stickers; the library's side list shows each heading as a box and the line shown in the accent, as its category bar does.
