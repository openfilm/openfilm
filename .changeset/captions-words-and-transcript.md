Studio: a caption on the picture is picked, framed and dragged as anything there is, with the plain pointer, and is shown whole while dragged out of its region. ⌘Z undoes a change made with a button or slider in the inspector right away. The caption inspector's Cut by transcript shows the track's words as text: words picked with a drag and Delete cut their picture and sound out of the film, everything after closed up. Animation starts with Word by word, a tile for each effect (color, appear, fade, rise, pop, blur, grow, bounce, flash, box, underline), each playing under the pointer, with the color it lights with; captions with no word times can have them estimated by their length, and a translation of words timed as said has its own words timed too.
