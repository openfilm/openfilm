Drag onto a clip to preview a replacement and confirm it, or drop near its edges to insert. The insertion preview follows the pointer even when a new track is needed for audio. Clips on locked tracks cannot be selected. Collapse track controls from the boundary and open them from each track's menu.

Keep thumbnails at their aspect ratio when zooming, show linked clips following the main track as it moves, and preview a marker's frame while the playhead stays put. Playback starts from the previewed marker.
