In the desktop app the media drawer runs down under the chat's composer, which floats over it with its own shadow. A voice's sample plays in the drawer's player, as sounds and music do. Every library card's star and "+" are round buttons of one size, and a voice row's end gets a deeper shade so they stand out. The featured mark on a voice is solid, and the drawer's saved media is called Favorites.
