Studio: images and slides are exported from any frames you pick, not only from the middle of each shot. The Images and Slides & PDF pages show the film large at one moment, drawn as the export will draw it (in the frame and fill chosen), over a strip of the film that zooms and scrolls like the timeline (Ctrl/⌘ + wheel or a pinch, a sideways swipe, or the zoom bar), down to a second across, with a ruler. A click finds a moment, a double click or Return adds it, and ← → step a frame. A frame picked is selected with a click: its dot shows on the strip, to drag, or Alt + ← → moves it a frame; a second click lets it go. Quick picks fill the list with the playhead, the middle of each shot, each marker, or one frame every few seconds. One image comes out as a file of its own, several in a zip; each still and slide is named by the shot it is in. The picks are remembered per project. The export dialog is larger, up to 1120 × 800.
