Canvas: moving a card moves the card, never the board — the board only glides to show a card's panel after a plain click. A group is held by its title bar, as in node editors: a drag there moves it with what it holds, a click picks it, and a drag anywhere inside it draws a selection box over its cards. The bar of list, map, wires, grid and zoom sits at the bottom right; the chat beside the canvas opens and folds from a layout button in the top bar, left of the Canvas button.
