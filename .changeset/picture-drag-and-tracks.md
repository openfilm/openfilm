Studio: dragging a layer on the picture of a heavy page keeps its frame with the picture: each step is answered once the page has drawn it, so the frame no longer runs ahead while the picture stands still. Bring to front / Send to back on a whole clip moves only that clip (onto a new top or bottom track when its track holds others), not its whole track and the clips beside it. Undoing a track reorder after the agent added or moved tracks puts back the track that moved, in its place. [ and ] pressed in the timeline select left / right of the playhead, even with a layer in hand.
