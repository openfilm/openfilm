Voice-over in two voices: a pair speaks a dialogue, written a turn a line. The voice library's voice-over category has Pairs beside Female and Male, with Doubao's podcast pairs and a way to choose any two Doubao voices; `openfilm get tts` takes two voices (`--voice a+b`, or `--voice` twice) and makes one recording whose transcript has a cue for each turn.
