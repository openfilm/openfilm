Motion: New layer (text, rectangle, ellipse, polygon, star, solid, or the picked layers grouped into one), each a page
of the library as big as the picture. A shape repeats like an animation editor's repeater (copies, offset, and each
copy's shift, turn and scale). A page layer's numbers show under Contents, above its Transform, each with a stopwatch
and keyframes, played inside the page by its `:root` override. Its other properties (words, colors) are in Motion's
Properties panel, and a press there keeps the layer picked.
