The desktop app has a browser: a Browser workbench (beta, off until you turn it on in Community) shows the web in tabs beside the chat. Sites are kept apart from the app and the computer: they have their own storage, cannot reach Studio, your projects or addresses on this computer and its network, ask you before using the camera, the microphone, notifications or the clipboard, and cannot get past a certificate that does not check. Downloads go where you pick, a file that can run is said to be one first, and every file kept is marked as come from the web.
