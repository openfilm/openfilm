What comes from the community is kept in bounds. A project's own skills reach no agent until you allow them in that project, after reading them whole (invisible characters and hidden comments are marked, scripts are listed), and a changed file asks again. A workbench's page reaches no network address but the hosts its `openfilm.json` declares under `permissions.network`, and its page says which. A skill made by anyone but OpenFilm that brings scripts asks before it is installed or tried, and an update that would take more (scripts, hosts, a page, a program) asks before it is taken. Anything OpenFilm withdraws stops on every computer the next time the community index is read, wherever a copy of it is, and says why. Community items can be reported from their page. `openfilm publish [folder]` checks a workbench or a skill as the community's review will, and `openfilm://community/<owner>/<name>` links open the app on that item.
