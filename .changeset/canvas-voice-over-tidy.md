Canvas: an audio node speaks a line too — voice-over, music or a sound effect, each with its own models — in a voice picked from the library's voices, heard before it is picked; a sound is shown as its waveform with one button to play it. Cards no longer lie on each other or out of their group: what an agent adds, a card that grows and a board that opens are laid clear, groups grow round what they hold, and a group's "Tidy up" lays it out in rows. Everything on the board is drawn however far it is zoomed out; a text node is written in with one click or by typing on it; the ⊕ beside a card is easier to hit. The canvas is marked Beta.
