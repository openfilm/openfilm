# Code of conduct

OpenFilm is made by people from many places, with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We want every one
of them to feel welcome here: in issues, pull requests, Discussions and anywhere else the project meets.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good faith. Most mistakes are misunderstandings; ask before you judge.
- Help newcomers. Everyone asked a first question once.
- No harassment of any kind: no insults, slurs or personal attacks, no unwelcome sexual attention, no threats, no
  publishing anyone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone makes you or others feel unwelcome, write to **support@openfilm.dev**. Say what happened and where, with
links if you have them. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, pull requests and other content that breaks this code, and may
warn, or block from the project, anyone who does.

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
